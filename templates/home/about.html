Our Story

From humble beginnings to a thriving yoga community

2010

Founded in Raipur

Started as a small studio with just 3 teachers and a vision to make authentic yoga accessible to everyone in Chhattisgarh.

2014

First Expansion

Opened our second location in Bhilai, serving over 500 students monthly with diverse yoga styles.

2018

Digital Transformation

Launched online classes, reaching students across India and establishing our teacher training program.

2023

Nimora Yogaverse Today

Now serving 10,000+ students with 50+ certified teachers across 5 locations and online platforms.
